## The Nearshoring Bottleneck
The nearshoring boom is rapidly transforming Hermosillo and the entire Sonora corridor. Manufacturing companies are investing millions in new plants and production lines to bring their goods closer to the US market. However, there is a critical bottleneck that is rarely discussed: transportation capacity.

Tomorrow, November 4th, our director Rogelio Romo will be at the Canacintra Hermosillo Expo talking directly with industry leaders. The main topic of discussion is clear: producing more is useless if your finished goods are stuck on a loading dock due to a lack of trucks.

## The Asset-Light Risk
Relying on digital, asset-light brokers to move your freight into the United States introduces a massive risk to your production quotas. These middlemen do not own the trucks. When demand spikes and the border gets congested, spot market drivers disappear and rates skyrocket. 

If your supply chain depends on a broker begging for space on public load boards, your Q4 deliverables are in jeopardy.
