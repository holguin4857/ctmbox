## The Month-End Push Through Nogales
July officially closes this Friday, and the operational realities in the Arizona-Sonora corridor are shifting rapidly. Manufacturers are aggressively pushing final end-of-month inventory through the Nogales port of entry to meet Q3 quotas. 

Whenever a month comes to a close, border capacity tightens. But right now, this surge in manufacturing volume is colliding with the lingering effects of summer driver shortages, creating a massive bottleneck for shippers trying to move high-value freight at the last minute. 

## The Trap of Asset-Light Brokers
If you are a mid-market shipper relying on a digital broker to secure capacity this week, you are walking directly into a spot market trap. 

When demand spikes at the end of the month, the open market becomes chaotic. Asset-light brokers, who have zero physical trucks of their own, are forced to scramble for leftover capacity. To protect their massive Fortune 500 accounts, these brokers will quietly prioritize enterprise freight, leaving mid-sized shippers to deal with exorbitant last-minute rate hikes or dropped loads. 

Operating on the open market at the end of the month means fighting for expensive, unvetted third-party trucks just to keep your production lines moving.
