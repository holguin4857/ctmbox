## The Industrial Boom in Sonora
The manufacturing ecosystem in Sonora is expanding at an unprecedented rate. To sustain this level of production, cross-border logistics must be flawless. On November 4th and 5th, industry leaders are gathering at the [Canacintra Hermosillo Industrial Expo](https://www.canacintrahermosillo.com/expo/), the premier event for strengthening supply chains in our region.

We understand that true logistics value lies not just in the trucks, but in the strategic relationships we build on both sides of the border.

## Connect with Rogelio Romo
While we won't have a physical booth on the exhibition floor this year, CTM will be heavily represented. Our founder and director, Rogelio Romo, is attending the event on Wednesday, November 4th.

Rogelio will be walking the floor, reconnecting with our Mexican colleagues, meeting new manufacturing partners, and talking face-to-face about current capacity and security challenges at the Nogales border. It is the perfect opportunity to discuss how a dedicated, asset-based fleet can eliminate the headaches of the volatile spot market.

## Let's Talk Cross-Border Solutions
If you are attending the Canacintra Expo, don't miss the chance to connect. Keep an eye out for him on the expo floor or reach out directly to schedule a quick coffee during the event.
