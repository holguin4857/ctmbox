## The September 16th Border Rush
Next Wednesday, September 16th, marks Mexican Independence Day. For cross-border supply chains in the Sonora-Arizona corridor, this major national holiday triggers a massive operational bottleneck. As manufacturing facilities and customs operations prepare to shut down or run on severely reduced hours, there is an aggressive, industry-wide sprint to move freight northbound before the deadline.

This sudden surge in volume instantly drains available capacity. When a holiday rush hits, the open spot market becomes highly volatile, resulting in skyrocketing rates and severe equipment shortages at the border. 

## The Broker Disconnect
