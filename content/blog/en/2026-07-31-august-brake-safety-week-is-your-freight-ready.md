## The August Regulatory Blitz
August begins tomorrow, which means the Commercial Vehicle Safety Alliance (CVSA) Brake Safety Week is imminent. During this annual enforcement blitz, Department of Transportation (DOT) inspectors across North America will aggressively target commercial vehicles, pulling thousands of trucks out of service for brake system violations.

For cross-border logistics in the Arizona-Sonora corridor, this week creates massive operational delays. If a truck hauling your freight fails a brake inspection at a weigh station, it is immediately sidelined. The vehicle cannot move until mobile mechanics are dispatched to perform on-site repairs, leaving your high-value inventory stranded and your production schedules completely derailed.
