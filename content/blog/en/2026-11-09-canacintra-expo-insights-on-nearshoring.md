## The Pulse of the Sonora Industry
Last week, our director Rogelio Romo attended the Canacintra Industrial Expo in Hermosillo. It was two days of intense meetings, reconnecting with industry peers, and dozens of face-to-face conversations with manufacturing leaders. The level of energy and investment in Sonora is historic thanks to nearshoring, but the meetings confirmed a constant concern dominating the market.

Factories are producing at record levels, but the logistics infrastructure required to cross those goods through Nogales is under extreme pressure.

## The Reality of Logistics Capacity
What Rogelio heard on the expo floor validates exactly what we see every day at the border. Supply chain managers are deeply frustrated with the open spot market. Relying on asset-light brokers to move high-value freight has become an unacceptable risk for Q4 production quotas.

When manufacturing facilities expand their assembly lines, they need guarantees that trucks and dry vans will be available to move their finished products. The empty promises of digital middlemen are no longer enough to sustain this pace of growth.
