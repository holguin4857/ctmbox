When you entrust your high-value freight to a fatigued driver, you risk your inventory and your reputation. Driver fatigue is a leading cause of highway accidents and severe delays. A driver pressured to deliver on time at the expense of their rest, or one skipping FMCSA Hours of Service (HOS) regulations, is not a cost-saving measure, it is a massive liability for your company.
